Working method

A simple process from need to handoff

The process stays proportionate to the request. Small tasks remain simple; larger work receives a more detailed scope.

Understand

Clarify the problem, users, context, constraints, and desired result.

Define

Agree on the deliverable, required access, timeframe, and evidence of completion.

Implement

Complete the work using an appropriate, maintainable approach and validate it.

Handoff

Provide the result, documentation where relevant, and practical next steps.

Clear expectations

A good engagement starts with the right fit

Transparent boundaries protect your systems, information, time, and the quality of the result.

Good fit

  • A specific problem or outcome can be described.
  • The necessary information and authorized access can be provided.
  • There is time to test, review, and confirm the result.
  • The request matches the services and evidence shown here.

Discuss first

  • Emergency or continuous 24/7 support requirements
  • Work involving sensitive information or regulated systems
  • Unauthorized access, surveillance, or security bypass requests
  • Large systems requiring a wider specialist team

Start with the need

Tell me what you need to solve

A short, clear description is enough for the first conversation. I will use it to determine whether the request fits and what information is needed next.

Include these three points:
  • What is happening now?
  • What result do you need?
  • When do you need it?
