Privacy and analytics

This portfolio offers optional, consent-based Google Analytics 4 measurement to understand aggregate page visits and portfolio interactions. It is off by default and is not used for advertising or personal profiling.

What is measured

Analytics remains off unless you explicitly allow it. If allowed, Google Analytics may use first-party analytics cookies to measure the page path, page language and broad actions such as viewing a résumé, opening a certificate, verifying a credential, visiting a case study or choosing a contact channel. Contact names, email addresses, messages and form contents are never sent to Analytics.

  • Analytics storage is denied by default and advertising storage always remains denied.
  • Google Signals and advertising personalization are disabled.
  • Query strings are removed from the page location sent by the portfolio.
  • Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track prevent the Google tag from loading.
  • No user ID, custom visitor ID or fingerprint is created by the portfolio.

Purpose, provider and choices

Aggregate measurements help evaluate whether portfolio evidence and contact paths are useful. Google processes the measurement as the service provider. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can change your choice at any time below. Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track always keeps analytics disabled in a compatible browser.
