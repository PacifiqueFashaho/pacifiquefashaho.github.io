## My story

## Learning by solving practical problems

My experience with technology is grounded in real, everyday needs. I have supported computer users, prepared and maintained Windows workstations, troubleshot printers and connectivity, assisted with digital services, and helped teams collect, validate, and report information.

Those experiences taught me that technology is not only about tools. It is about understanding what someone needs, identifying the cause of a problem, choosing a safe and practical response, and confirming that the result works.

As my experience developed, I became interested in connecting three disciplines: IT Support, Software Development, and Data Analytics. Support helps me understand users and systems. Development helps me turn ideas into usable solutions. Data Analytics helps me organize evidence, identify patterns, and communicate decisions clearly.

This portfolio documents that continuing path honestly. Professional experience, representative workflows, implemented projects, and learning case studies are identified separately so visitors can understand exactly what each example proves.

## How I approach my work

### Understand before acting

I start by clarifying the problem, its impact, and the evidence available. A fast answer is not useful if it addresses the wrong cause.

### Make the work understandable

I value clear explanations, documented decisions, and practical handoffs. Technical work should remain understandable to the person using or maintaining it.

### Validate the result

I do not treat an action as complete only because a command ran or a page loaded. I check whether the expected result is present and whether the user’s need has actually been addressed.

### Keep learning honestly

I distinguish professional experience from representative examples and learning projects. This helps me show progress without overstating what the evidence proves.

### Respect people and privacy

I aim to collect only the information a task needs, protect personal details, and avoid exposing data through documentation, analytics, or public examples.
