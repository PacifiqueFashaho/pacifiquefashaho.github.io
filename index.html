About me

Computer science grounded in real problems

My path in computer science is shaped by a simple idea: useful technology connects sound engineering with real human needs.

I have supported computer users and systems, built web and Python solutions, and helped teams collect, validate, and communicate data. These experiences developed my ability to move between people, systems, software, and information without losing sight of the problem being solved.

I approach technical work by clarifying the need, breaking complexity into manageable parts, implementing carefully, and validating the result. I value solutions that another person can understand, maintain, and trust.
