My story

Learning by solving practical problems

My experience with technology is grounded in real, everyday needs.

I have supported computer users, prepared and maintained Windows workstations, troubleshot printers and connectivity, assisted with digital services, and helped teams collect, validate, and report information.

Those experiences taught me that technology is not only about tools. It is about understanding what someone needs, identifying the cause, choosing a safe and practical response, and confirming that the result works.

This portfolio documents that continuing path honestly. Professional experience, representative workflows, implemented projects, and learning case studies are identified separately so visitors can understand what each example proves.
